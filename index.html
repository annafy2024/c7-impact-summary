Why C7, Why Now

I joined Shopify on March 3rd, 2024 as a Senior Partner Solutions Engineer, already focusing primarily on the B2B ecosystem. On July 1st, 2025, I transitioned to the B2B SE team as a Senior Solutions Engineer—a move that formalized my deep commitment to building Shopify's B2B presence. Since Q3 2025, I've been the technical leader for B2B acquisition across EMEA and APAC—a scope that requires navigating vastly different market dynamics, purchasing behaviors, and regulatory environments.

Rather than simply covering territory, I've used this position to build scalable infrastructure: reusable tools used across teams globally, hands-on onboarding that accelerated two new SEs to successfully pass their mock demos and formal onboarding, and a clear POV on the product and market that's now referenced by Partner SEs across regions.

The impact is measurable: 335% of target in Q4, five midmarket launches with strong product-market fit successfully delivered, and Partner SE teams equipped with assets they actively use in merchant conversations. But the strategic value extends beyond individual deals. I've established systematic feedback loops with Product, validated gray-area solutions by connecting prospects with engineering, and represented Shopify's B2B + AI capabilities at external industry events—building credibility in markets where we're still establishing our enterprise presence.

What makes this C7-level work: I'm not just executing in a region—I'm defining how we win in these markets. I've built the foundation of the Partner SE function in EMEA, giving me unique relationships that accelerate deals and enable cross-functional collaboration. I take on leadership responsibilities our North America-based Solutions Engineering leads can't address due to timezone and cultural context, from coaching new SEs on European sales cycles to advising on regional go-to-market strategy.

My connections run deep: internally with Product, Partnerships, and Sales Leadership; externally with the partner ecosystem that implements our most complex deals. I understand the nuances that matter—German procurement processes differ from French, APAC buying committees operate differently than European, and launch timelines vary significantly by market maturity. This isn't just regional knowledge; it's the strategic context that helps us scale efficiently and avoid costly missteps.

C7 recognizes SEs who are business multipliers, not just quota achievers. I've demonstrated this by making the team more capable, the product roadmap more informed, our partner ecosystem more enabled, and our B2B positioning sharper. The scope I've managed, the impact I've delivered, and the infrastructure I've built position Shopify to scale B2B successfully across EMEA and APAC—that's the return on a C7 investment.
